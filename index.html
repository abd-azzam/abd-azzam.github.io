{{ site.author.name }} is an M.A. student in Political Science at Universitas Islam Internasional Indonesia (UIII). Azzam's thesis, supervised by Sirojuddin Arif, asks whether deindustrialization breeds state capitalism.

It follows the B.A. thesis in Sociology at UIN Syarif Hidayatullah Jakarta (2025), supervised by Prof. Iim Halimatusa'diyah, which estimated the effect of premature deindustrialization on environmental degradation in 43 Muslim countries between 2009 and 2023.

Azzam is also a graduate research assistant at UIII. One project, funded by the Ministry of Religious Affairs, uses INDO-DAPOER data on more than 600 Indonesian cities to estimate how regional development affects the presence of vocational high schools (SMK). The other, for KAICIID, studies interfaith cooperation on the environment through interviews with faith-based communities in Yogyakarta and Jakarta and a survey built on Elinor Ostrom's Governing the Commons.

Before UIII, Azzam interned at J-PAL Southeast Asia, assembled city-level data for a Ministry of Tourism and Creative Economy project at Next Policy, and analyzed fieldwork interviews for PPIM at UIN Jakarta. Most of the work is in Stata, R, and Python.
