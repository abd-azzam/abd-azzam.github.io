About me

{{ site.author.name }} is a graduate student in Political Science at Universitas Islam Internasional Indonesia (UIII). Azzam's work uses quantitative and qualitative methods to study development, the environment, and education.

At UIII, Azzam is a graduate research assistant on KAICIID's research project on interfaith collaboration on the environment, built on Elinor Ostrom's Governing the Commons framework. Earlier research posts include J-PAL Southeast Asia, Next Policy, and the Center for the Study of Islam and Society (PPIM) UIN Jakarta. Currently he also serves as Graduate Fellow at AI, Economics, and Policy Analytics (AIEPA) Lab of UIII .

Azzam holds a B.A. in Sociology from Syarif Hidayatullah State Islamic University Jakarta (2025). The undergraduate thesis examined the effect of premature deindustrialization on environmental degradation in 43 Muslim countries between 2009 and 2023.
