Claim Viewer

A PDF reader for claims and notes. Windows app, or any Chromium browser. MIT licensed.

Zotero keeps my PDFs and highlights, but it doesn't draw how the claims across my readings connect, and it has no place for my own reading notes. So this does the reading, the tagging, the notes and the graph in one place.

Select a passage and save it as a claim, tag it, and link it to claims in other readings. Write Zettelkasten-style notes under nested tags, file your PDFs in folders the way Zotero does, and see how it all fits together as a graph.

It runs on your own computer, so your PDFs, claims and notes are never uploaded. The only thing that goes online is an optional dictionary lookup of a single word. Built with Claude Code, under my supervision.
