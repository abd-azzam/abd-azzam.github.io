Claim Viewer

A desktop app for reading papers, with claims, notes and Word citations. Windows 10 and 11. MIT licensed.

Zotero keeps my PDFs and highlights, but it doesn't draw how the claims across my readings connect, and it has no place for my own reading notes. Word can make a citation, but not the sentence the citation rests on. So this does the reading, the tagging, the notes, the graph, the AI chat and the Word citations in one place.

Select a passage and save it as a claim, tag it, and link it to claims in other readings. Write Zettelkasten-style notes under nested tags, file your PDFs in folders the way Zotero does, and see how it all fits together as a graph. You can also ask an AI about the PDF, using your own provider, and the points in its answers link to the page they came from. Citations and a bibliography go into desktop Word from a ribbon tab, in APSA and other styles.

It runs on your own computer, and reading and organising work offline. The parts you switch on yourself do use the internet: Ask AI sends the text you ask about to the provider you connect, and DOI and dictionary lookups go online too. Built with Claude Code, Hermes (connected to DeepSeek Flash) and GPT Sol 6.1, under my supervision.
