Claim Viewer

A PDF reader for claims. Windows app, or any Chromium browser. MIT licensed.

Zotero keeps my PDFs and highlights, but it doesn't draw how the claims across my readings connect, and I didn't want to keep switching back to Obsidian while reading. So this does the reading, the tagging and the graph in one place: select a passage, save it as a claim, tag it by pathway, cause, outcome and direction, then see the claims laid out as a graph.

It runs on your own computer, so your PDFs and claims are never uploaded. Built with Claude Code, under my supervision.
