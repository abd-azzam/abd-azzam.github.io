Current projects

Skill Formation and Vocational Schooling in Indonesia

With Sirojuddin Arif. Universitas Islam Internasional Indonesia, funded by the Ministry of Religious Affairs, 2026.

Poisson regression on INDO-DAPOER data across 600+ Indonesian cities, estimating the effect of regional development on the presence of vocational high schools (SMK); literature reviews on skill formation and on STEM in Malaysian and Indonesian higher education.

With Testriono and Khaidir Hasram. KAICIID research, Universitas Islam Internasional Indonesia, 2026–present.

Survey instrument built on Elinor Ostrom's Governing the Commons framework; in-depth interviews with faith-based communities in Yogyakarta and Jakarta.
