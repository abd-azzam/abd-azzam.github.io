Research interests

  • Deindustrialization and state capitalism
  • Deindustrialization and the environment in Muslim-majority countries
  • Vocational education and skill formation in Indonesia
  • Faith-based communities and environmental cooperation
  • Decentralization in Indonesia

Theses

  2. The Effect of Premature Deindustrialization on Environmental Degradation in 43 Muslim Countries during 2009–2023.
    B.A. thesis, Sociology, Syarif Hidayatullah State Islamic University Jakarta, 2025. Supervised by Prof. Iim Halimatusa'diyah, Ph.D.

Current projects

  1. Skill Formation and Vocational Schooling in Indonesia, with Sirojuddin Arif.
    Universitas Islam Internasional Indonesia, funded by the Ministry of Religious Affairs, 2026. Poisson regression on INDO-DAPOER data across 600+ Indonesian cities, estimating the effect of regional development on the presence of vocational high schools (SMK); literature reviews on skill formation and on STEM in Malaysian and Indonesian higher education.
  2. Interfaith Collaboration on the Environment, with Testriono and Khaidir Hasram.
    KAICIID research, Universitas Islam Internasional Indonesia, 2026–present. Survey instrument built on Elinor Ostrom's Governing the Commons framework; in-depth interviews with faith-based communities in Yogyakarta and Jakarta.
